### 1. The held state (commit `f974fee`)

`SH2_CastChannel` enters `SH2_Channel_State` (id 746006) in both `1hm_behavior` and
`magicbehavior`. Its `MODE_LOOPING` generator sits on `Animations\1HM_Shout_Inhale.HKX` and has no
end-of-clip trigger, so the state is held until `SH2_CastExit` at release. Before this, a channel
played a single-play throw clip, exited at ~1.8s, and the rest of the hold was the OAR idle
replacement — the owner's "2 animations play" and the lingering arm.

`1HM_Shout_Inhale.HKX` is the only clip all 22 non-idle concentration submods replace, so one
generator covers every family and OAR still picks per-family from global `815`.

**Trap:** `eventNames` and `eventInfos` are parallel arrays. Adding an event to `#0085` / `#0077`
needs a matching entry in `#0087` / `#0079`, or Nemesis fails BOTH graphs at serialization with
`ERROR(1003)` while naming only one.

### 2. The driver entry (commit `7947901`)

`begin()` picks the entry from the shape. Fire-and-forget still walks `MSCO_left1..4` by the cast
index; a channel sends its own event. Also gated the `SH2_CastExit` "press produced no payload"
warning on fire-and-forget, because a channel legitimately exits without a SpellFire — it commits
on ADR-0006's authored cast-time floor instead, since the concentration clips carry no annotation.

### 3. The hold credit (commit `42e9d28`)

`RollingMcoCombo::kMaxAgeMs` is 5000ms, sized for a throw clip. A channel is unbounded and takes
no sample while our state is live, so any hold past five seconds aged the pre-hold sample out and
armed nothing. The hold is credited back at release. A gap BEFORE the hold still ages out.

### 4. The combo index — the part that is not finished (commits `8759998`, `adb8d69`)

Measured live over a four-hit chain with a temporary probe on thirteen graph edges:

```
MCO_WinClose -> 1 │ AttackInitiate -> 1   (attack1 plays)
MCO_WinClose -> 2 │ AttackInitiate -> 2   (attack2 plays)
MCO_WinClose -> 3 │ AttackInitiate -> 3   (attack3 plays)
attackStop   -> 1                          (chain ends, resets)
```

`MCO_nextattack` at attack time is the swing ALREADY PLAYING; at `MCO_WinClose` it is the swing
that comes NEXT. Sampling at attack time therefore restored the current index and repeated it —
the owner's `a1 -> a2 -> hold -> a2`. Three corrections went in:

- sample at `MCO_WinClose`, not `MCO_AttackInitiate` / `HitFrame`
- do NOT sample `attackStop` — it is MCO's reset to 1, the stomp the rolling cache exists to
  outlive (ticket 11 says this outright)
- while a restore is pending, a `WinClose` is a stomp to put back, not a value to learn from; a
  post-cast reset was both teaching 1 and dropping the pending restore, because `record()` clears
  it on the assumption that a real SWING invalidates one

**Ticket 11's "preserve, never derive" is intact.** `sample + 1` was considered and rejected: the
ticket's own evidence is that moveset length lives in the clip annotations, so deriving runs off
the end of a shorter moveset.

**NOT VERIFIED. The owner's call, 2026-08-24: the approach is too inconsistent and needs
revisiting, possibly a full refactor. Do not build further on it.**

```
Animations\MCO_attack2.hkx   Clip: MCO_ClipGenerator_MCO_attack2

```

Two further observations from that log, recorded because they were not part of the model this
session worked from:

- The attack clips are supplied by **`Nolvus OAR Stance Combat Framework`, submod `Sword Neutral`**,
  replacing `Animations\MCO_attackN.hkx` on generators named `MCO_ClipGenerator_MCO_attackN`. The
  state that runs picks the generator; OAR then picks the file. An OAR stance framework sits
  between `MCO_nextattack` and the clip the player sees.
- Only `MCO_attack1` and `MCO_attack2` appear anywhere in that log. Whether this stance's moveset
  is two hits long was not established. If it is, an index of 3 is out of range for it -- and the
  four-hit chain measured earlier this session was taken under a different weapon and stance, so
  the two measurements may not describe the same moveset at all.

**The verification method used in this session was invalid and must not be reused.** It read
`MCO_nextattack` at `MCO_AttackInitiate` and treated a value of 3 as "attack3 played". The OAR log
shows the attack-2 clip loading in the same run. The graph variable and the clip OAR actually
loads can disagree, so the variable proves nothing about which attack ran. The project's own rule
already covers this -- a visual claim needs the frame, or here the Animation Log -- and it was not
applied. **The Animation Log is the authority for which clip played.** Anything the next session
concludes about combo position must be checked against it, not against a graph read.

This also means the three corrections in this commit are unproven, not merely incomplete. They are
individually well-evidenced about WHERE MCO writes the variable (the four-hit chain measurement
above stands, and was itself read from the probe rather than assumed), but writing that variable
back evidently does not determine the clip that follows. That gap is the thing to investigate.

Note the change also altered sampling for **fire-and-forget casts**, not just channels -- wider
than this ticket's scope, and the failures above are on the fire-and-forget path. Reverting
commits `8759998` and `adb8d69` returns sampling to attack-time events; the pre-existing behaviour
was also wrong, so a revert restores a different failure, not a working state.

### What a refactor already knows

`questions/question-2026-08-12-sh2-to-shoutmco-combo-index-ownership.md` is **answered**, and it
closes the obvious escape route:

- ShoutMCO never reads or writes `MCO_nextattack` for an SH2 driver cast. Its arm is exactly
  `SBF_ShoutStart`, which a post-ticket-08 cast never raises. SH2 must own this itself.
- The two writers cannot collide.
- **"No extra seam. Write the variables directly."** So "ask ShoutMCO to own it" is not available;
  its API answers whether a press may start, not where a cast sits in the chain.

Ticket 11 also records that MCO's `AttackNodes_StateMachine` binds its `startStateId` to
`MCO_nextattack` through an `hkbVariableBindingSet`. If that binding is the selector, writing the
variable should choose the state -- and the observations say it does not, at least not at the
moment we write. Whether the state machine latches `startStateId` at a different time than we
assume is the first thing worth measuring, against the Animation Log rather than the variable.

## New scope the owner raised, not yet ticketed

**Dual-cast fire-and-forget plays the left-hand animation.** The owner's Animation Log shows
`SH2_CastRight_Clip` / `SH2_Cast2..4_Clip` all resolving to `MSCO_left1..4.hkx`. The `shtb`
generators hardcode the left set, so a dual cast plays a one-handed clip. The owner wants MSCO's
**dual-cast** animations. MCBO ships `MSCO_dual1..10.hkx` for exactly this.

Likely shape: dual clip generators and states on `Animations\MSCO_dual1..4.hkx`, plus a driver
entry chosen when the cast is dual — the same pattern as the channel state. No new assets; MCBO
already ships the clips. This is a separate ticket, not part of 28.

Related and already known: the fork's fire-and-forget casts use MCBO's clips, not Spell Hotbar
2's own. Upstream's F&F cast submods (`cast_1h_left`, `cast_dual`, `cast_ritual`) sit on the shout
paths and the fork bypasses all of them. That is a deliberate trade — MCO's annotations
(`MLh_SpellFire_Event`, `MSCO_WinClose`) live in the same file as the pose, so you get MCO timing
or SH2 per-family art, not both, without re-annotating clips. Nobody wrote that trade down; the
five empty `cast_left_1000x` submod folders look like an abandoned attempt to revisit it.

## How to test without the owner

Most of this ticket was driven unattended. Worth knowing:

- **Attacks:** DevBench `input`, `device:"mouse"`, `key:0`, `userEvent:"Right Attack/Block"`,
  `action:"hold"`, `holdSeconds:0.2`. A `tap` is too short and silently does nothing.
- **Casts:** `papyrus action=call script=SpellHotbar function=castSlot args=[N]`. Slot 0 is a
  concentration spell, slot 3 is fire-and-forget. `GetKeyBind(N)` returns the scan code —
  slots 0-11 are Numpad (79, 80, 81, 75, 76, 77, 71, 72, 73, 82, 181, 55).
- **The limit:** injected key events do NOT reach SH2's hotbar handler, and `castSlot` starts a
  channel that dies in ~4ms because `m_keybind.isDown()` is false. **A real held key is the only
  way to test a channel**, so the channel combo cell needs the owner.
- Console capture returns nothing in this setup; read `SpellHotbar2.log` instead.
- A cast fired mid-swing is refused and deferred to ShoutMCO, landing ~1s later. Allow for it.
