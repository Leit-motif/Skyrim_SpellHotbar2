## Evidence and interpretation

- The 1.666667-second dual-wield animation advances roughly 284 units.
- Both weapon nodes deliver a paired hit at 0.583333 seconds and another paired hit at 0.883333
  seconds; the second pair carries the `HitFrame.$AoW_Knockback` payload.
- The approved glyph resolves the action as a leaping Nord barbarian performing a simultaneous
  inward cross-cut with two compact one-handed axes.
- Each red trail is visibly generated by an axe blade and follows that blade's curved swing path.
  The crossed energy is therefore a record of weapon motion rather than an independent emblem or
  radial effect.

## Approved visual result

The final icon depicts one airborne, blond-haired Nord barbarian in rough fur and hide. His crossed
arms drive exactly two inward-facing one-handed axes through one simultaneous cross-cut. Two
crimson energy trails remain tangent to the cutting edges and curve backward along the axes' lines
of motion. A dark smoky field keeps the figure, axes, red eyes, and crossed blade trails readable as
an MMO hotbar glyph.
