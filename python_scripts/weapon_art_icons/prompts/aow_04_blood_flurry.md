## Evidence and interpretation

- `AABL_Attack_A.HKX` is 9 seconds long with twelve hit frames.
- The last hit invokes a downward-smash effect, jump-attack sound, rim shake, hit fog, and heavy
  paralysis.
- Its OAR condition restricts the art to greatswords.
- The Orc-coded shock-trooper silhouette follows the owner's race/fighting-style guidance.
- Three broad crimson arcs compress the long flurry into hotbar-readable visual shorthand; the
  lower-right impact represents the terminal slam.

## Final prompt

```text
Create ONE original square RPG hotbar ability icon for the weapon art “Blood Flurry.”

REFERENCE ROLES:
The three references are approved project style anchors only. Match their compact square MMO spell-icon grammar, generalized faceless dark silhouette, bold action, limited palette, atmospheric bloom, safe crop, and readability at 32x32. Do not reuse their poses, figures, weapons, effects, or compositions.

EVIDENCE-BASED SUBJECT:
The actual animation is a nine-second GREATSWORD-only sequence with twelve rapid hit frames, ending in a heavy downward paralysis slam. Depict one generalized Orc-coded shock trooper silhouette in a low, brutal greatsword stance. Use only economical Orc cues: unusually broad powerful frame, heavy angular shoulder silhouette, and perhaps the faintest suggestion of short lower tusks in profile; no face or portrait detail. The figure is caught at the decisive final phase of a sustained flurry, both hands bringing a massive simple greatsword downward at a steep diagonal.

ABILITY EFFECT:
Surround the sword and upper body with THREE broad overlapping crimson slash afterimages, arranged tightly enough to imply many preceding blows without drawing twelve separate marks. All arcs funnel into ONE bright blood-red impact burst at the lower-right ground point. The visual story is relentless repeated cutting resolving into a crushing final slam. Use abstract red energy resembling blood in color and weight, but no gore, wounds, severed anatomy, droplets, or realistic liquid. Keep the flurry compact around the figure; do not turn it into a full circular cyclone.

STYLE:
Original fantasy MMO hotbar glyph/icon, not character key art and not a detailed weapon illustration. Faceless near-black silhouette, simple greatsword shape, bold large masses, clean negative space, restrained painted texture. Limited palette: black/deep oxblood field, saturated crimson arcs, narrow white-red cutting edges, tiny dull-orange impact sparks. Elder Scrolls race and weapon lore guide the silhouette, but no Bethesda or third-party art may be copied. No armor ornament, detailed blade engraving, individual fingers, scenery, text, letters, runes, logos, watermark, or UI border. Keep all important shapes safely inset. It must read at 32x32 as: broad warrior + greatsword + repeated red slashes + finishing impact.

DO NOT recreate the earlier rejected Blood Flurry pilot: no isolated ornate sword, no transparent background, no detailed weapon rendering, no promotional splash-art composition.

CELL 5 CONSTRAINT: entirely original generated artwork; no third-party art, copied motifs, traced poses, or reproduced game assets.
```
