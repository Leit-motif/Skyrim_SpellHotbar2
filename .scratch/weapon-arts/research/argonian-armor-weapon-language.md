# Argonian Armor and Weapon Language for Leap Slam

Status: bounded visual research for revising the `Leap Slam` MMO hotbar icon. This is prompt guidance, not permission to copy shipped art. ESO references below are official Elder Scrolls material used as cross-series Argonian cultural evidence; the final icon should remain an original Skyrim-style synthesis.

## Confirmed official evidence

### Dead-Water combat language

The official *Murkmire* page publishes a ZeniMax concept sheet titled **“Arms and armor of the fierce Dead-Water tribe.”** Its combat silhouettes use:

- overlapping reptile-scale, scute, or shell-like plates rather than smooth medieval plate;
- dark slate/blue-black armor surfaces over moss or olive cloth, with ochre-brown scale accents;
- leather belts, wrapped joints, lashings, bone or tooth points, and sparse spikes;
- long wooden hafts with wrapped grips;
- asymmetric crescent, hook, and serrated weapon profiles, often with back-spines or claw-like terminals.

The same official page publishes **“Dead-Water hunters training with wamasu spears.”** Several Argonian fighters are explicitly bare-headed: their natural horns, crown spines, crests, scales, and face coloration supply the head silhouette. Their field gear is lean and mobile—sleeveless dark olive/charcoal cloth or leather, segmented scale panels, pale bone/ivory shoulder pieces or trophies, and long two-handed wooden shafts with broad leaf- or harpoon-like metal heads. This is direct support for an uncovered-head warrior rather than a helmeted generic lizardman.

Sources: [official Murkmire DLC page](https://www.elderscrollsonline.com/en-us/updates/dlc/murkmire); [official Dead-Water arms-and-armor concept sheet](https://esossl-a.akamaihd.net/assets/img/cms/media/f9e57aa397cbbf0e38da1a98cb614cde_arms-and-armor-of-the-fierce-deadwater-tribe_original.jpg); [official bare-headed Dead-Water training screenshot](https://esossl-a.akamaihd.net/assets/img/cms/media/3d2b290ca22ac19bab2692fe7ef2dd07_deadwater-hunters-training-with-wamasu-spears_original.jpg).

### Elder Argonian ceremonial alternative

The official Elder Argonian concept sheet uses a more refined, ceremonial vocabulary: bronze or aged-gold fittings over brown leather and deep-purple textiles; rib- or bone-like collar elements; stepped geometric ornament; turquoise-blue cabochons; restrained feathers; wrapped shafts; and angular, asymmetric weapon heads. It also shows the face and natural head crest left visible beneath open circlets or brow ornaments rather than enclosed helmets.

This is useful as a secondary accent source, but its bright gold, jewelry, and feathered regalia would make `Leap Slam` read more ceremonial than the Dead-Water field-warrior language.

Source: [official Elder Argonian outfits-and-weapons concept sheet](https://esossl-a.akamaihd.net/assets/img/cms/media/8920cdf5d9a45dbf85ea60075e40caf2_outfits-and-weapons-in-the-elder-argonian-style_original.jpg), hosted by the [official Murkmire DLC page](https://www.elderscrollsonline.com/en-us/updates/dlc/murkmire).

### Cultural materials and patterning

An official ZOS Crown Store post names flax, feathers, alit scales, and spun “dank-cotton” as Argonian clothing materials, arranged in contrasting chevrons and lightning patterns. That evidence concerns civilian cultural clothing, not battle armor, but it supports natural swamp materials and a small amount of angular textile patterning.

Source: [official ZOS post, “New Argonian Cultural Costumes Available in the Crown Store”](https://forums.elderscrollsonline.com/en/discussion/390672/new-argonian-cultural-costumes-available-in-the-crown-store-all-platforms-jan-18).

The official *Murkmire* patch notes independently identify **Crocodile Leather** as the Dead-Water style material and **Hackwing Plumage** as the Elder Argonian style material. This confirms that crocodilian leather and feathers are literal material anchors for those two motifs, not generic “tribal” invention.

Source: [official ESO PC/Mac Patch Notes v4.2.5](https://forums.elderscrollsonline.com/en/discussion/comment/5552032/).

## Official feathered headwear study

### Elder Argonian open circlets

The official Elder Argonian concept sheet shows three related open-head constructions, not enclosed helmets:

- a narrow deep-purple brow band with aged-bronze side fittings and a round forehead medallion, carrying a small cluster of about four visible pale tan feathers angled up and back;
- a narrow purple band with a central turquoise cabochon and round bronze temple discs, carrying a fan of about six visible cream, rust, and brown feathers swept rearward from the crown;
- a purple brow band beneath a tall, narrow bronze forehead plaque, with about five visible rust-and-cream feathers flaring backward from the sides.

The visible feather counts are observations of the published front/three-quarter concept sheet; overlapping feathers make the hidden attachment count unknowable. In every variant, the band stays at or above the brow. The Argonian snout, mouth, jaw, throat, and most of the scaled cranium remain uncovered. The plumage continues the natural rearward head silhouette rather than forming a brim or surrounding the face. The denser feather fans visually replace or merge with the natural crest, so the official art does **not** support adding both a huge horn crown and a huge feather crown at once.

Source: [official Elder Argonian outfits-and-weapons concept sheet](https://esossl-a.akamaihd.net/assets/img/cms/media/8920cdf5d9a45dbf85ea60075e40caf2_outfits-and-weapons-in-the-elder-argonian-style_original.jpg), hosted by the [official Murkmire DLC page](https://www.elderscrollsonline.com/en-us/updates/dlc/murkmire).

### Xanmeer Doyen's Feather Tiara

The official Crown Store image shows a more theatrical priestly construction. A broad, shallow, beaded brow panel sits above the eyes; on the Argonian display it follows the forehead without covering the long snout, cheeks, mouth, or jaw. A large half-fan of long dark olive and brown feathers radiates upward and backward from the crown. This is closer to an open feathered skullcap/tiara than a helmet, but the tall radial fan is intentionally ceremonial and much larger than the Elder Argonian combat-scale feather clusters.

ZOS describes the item as Xanmeer Priesthood regalia and specifically says its plumage waves “above your brow.” The official image therefore confirms the placement: brow-mounted, face open, feathers above and behind—not a feather mask, hood, full cap, or side-hanging warbonnet.

Sources: [official ZOS Crown Store announcement and product image](https://forums.elderscrollsonline.com/en/discussion/407926/new-costumes-hat-amp-motif-available-in-the-crown-store-all-platforms-april-19); [official Xanmeer Doyen's Feather Tiara image](https://esosslfiles-a.akamaihd.net/cms/2018/04/2ce6a36745f3a7874d1af7cb7845d93c.jpg).

### Leap Slam headwear recommendation

Use the compact Elder Argonian form, not the full Xanmeer priestly fan: one open deep-purple brow circlet with a small aged-bronze forehead fitting, one restrained turquoise stone, and **exactly five** medium feathers in muted cream, rust, and dark brown. Attach the feathers along the rear half of the circlet and sweep all five backward/upward with the leap. Keep the snout, jaw, throat, and scaled sides of the head completely exposed. Allow only one or two short natural side horns to remain visible below or between the feather roots; do not add a second large natural crest behind the feather fan.

At hotbar scale, the exact five-feather fan should read as one tapered rearward wedge. Avoid a full radial halo, vertical carnival plume, feather mohawk, hanging side feathers, face paint substituted for headwear, skull mask, metal dome, leather skullcap, hood, or closed helmet.

Compact model-facing headwear block:

> No helmet. One open Elder Argonian brow circlet: a narrow deep-purple band above the eyes with one small aged-bronze forehead fitting and one restrained turquoise cabochon; exactly five medium cream, rust, and dark-brown feathers fixed along the rear half of the band and swept backward/upward by the leap. Long reptilian snout, mouth, jaw, throat, and scaled side profile fully exposed; only one or two short natural side horns visible, no second crest, skullcap, mask, hood, radial halo, or towering ceremonial plume.

## Prompt recommendations for the Leap Slam revision

Use the action figure, not a costume portrait:

- **Head:** no helmet, hood, skullcap, or face-covering plate. Use either a fully bare natural horn-and-spine crown or the compact five-feather open circlet specified above; for the requested feathered revision, use the circlet. Keep the snout and jaw exposed and facial detail economical so the leap-slam action remains primary.
- **Armor silhouette:** lean Dead-Water heavy skirmisher rather than a bulky knight—segmented dark shell/scute chest plates, one or two pale bone shoulder guards, bracers, leather belt/lashings, open or lightly covered arms, and a clearly visible balancing tail.
- **Materials:** matte slate or blue-black shell/scale, weathered brown leather, wrapped marshwood, bone/ivory fasteners, moss/olive cloth, and small ochre accents. A restrained deep-purple or turquoise detail may nod to Elder Argonian craft, but should not become glowing magic.
- **Weapon:** exactly one connected two-handed Argonian poleaxe. Use one long dark marshwood haft with two clearly separated hand grips, leather wrapping, and one compact asymmetric crescent axe head with a short rear hook or back-spines. Bone lashings or a claw-like butt cap are appropriate. Keep the head practical and readable; do not turn it into a hammer, halberd bouquet, crystalline staff, or detached blade.
- **Motifs at 32 px:** prioritize three readable masses: bare horned Argonian head, overlapping shell/bone armor, and hooked crescent poleaxe. Fine chevrons, beads, feathers, and turquoise stones should be omitted or reduced to one accent.

Avoid generic medieval plate, polished steel knight armor, a closed shell helmet, crocodile-monster bulk, feather-headdress overload, Aztec/Mesoamerican shorthand, or generic fantasy “tribal” ornament. None of those is needed to make the figure read as an Elder Scrolls Argonian.

## Compact model-facing language

> Bare-headed Argonian Dead-Water heavy skirmisher, natural crown horns and short crest visible, lean segmented slate shell-and-scale armor over moss-olive cloth, weathered brown leather lashings, sparse pale bone shoulder and fastener accents, visible balancing tail; exactly one connected two-handed poleaxe with a long wrapped marshwood haft, two clear grips, and one compact asymmetric crescent blade with a short rear hook. Practical Elder Scrolls Black Marsh construction, no helmet and no generic medieval plate.
